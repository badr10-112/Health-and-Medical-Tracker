📰 Crohn's Research & Updates

A snapshot of recent clinical research, gathered July 2026. General information, not medical advice — always discuss changes with your gastroenterologist.

  • Guselkumab (Tremfya) approved for Crohn's. The IL-23 inhibitor gained FDA approval for moderately-to-severely active Crohn's in adults, expanding the targeted-biologic options beyond older anti-TNF drugs like infliximab. AJMC
  • GRAVITI: first IL-23 with a self-injection start. The Phase 3 GRAVITI trial met all endpoints and makes guselkumab the first IL-23 therapy offering a subcutaneous (under-the-skin) induction option instead of infusions. American College of Gastroenterology
  • Three IL-23 inhibitors now available. With mirikizumab and guselkumab joining risankizumab, clinicians now have a full class of IL-23 blockers to choose from for Crohn's. GI & Hepatology News
  • A new target — TL1A. Tulisokibart, an antibody against the TL1A protein, showed meaningful remission in a Phase 2 trial, pointing to a mechanism different from all current drugs. Cedars-Sinai
  • Stem-cell therapy for perianal fistulas. Darvadstrocel (mesenchymal stem cells) was tested in the Phase 3 ADMIRE-CD II trial for complex perianal fistulas — the first cell therapy approved for this hard-to-treat complication. Gastroenterology (PMC)
  • Cell therapy on the horizon. A 2026 review maps the pipeline from mesenchymal stem cells toward engineered CAR-T immune cells for IBD — early, but a possible future direction. Nature Reviews Gastroenterology & Hepatology
  • New oral candidate: obefazimod. The oral drug is in the Phase 2b ENHANCE-CD trial for people who haven't responded to conventional or advanced therapies. Novel-drugs pipeline review (PMC)
  • Head-to-head biologics. Risankizumab is being compared directly against ustekinumab, alongside a deep pipeline (icotrokinra, MORF-057 and others) advancing through trials. DelveInsight Pipeline 2026
  • Oral upadacitinib, including in children. The oral JAK inhibitor is being studied in moderate-to-severe Crohn's, with trials now extending to pediatric patients aged 2–18. UCSF IBD Trials
  • Combination biologic strategies. Trials are testing two biologics together (e.g. vedolizumab with adalimumab or ustekinumab) to see whether combinations control disease better than a single drug. UC Davis Crohn's Trials

Your drug — infliximab: how it compares & what's new

  • Infliximab now comes as a self-injection. Zymfentra (infliximab-dyyb) is the first subcutaneous infliximab — after IV induction, maintenance can be a home injection instead of infusions. Center for Biosimilars
  • Evidence behind subcutaneous infliximab. The Phase 3 LIBERTY-CD and LIBERTY-UC trials showed subcutaneous infliximab beat placebo for maintaining remission after IV induction. Managed Healthcare Executive
  • Biosimilars keep infliximab affordable. Versions like Inflectra, Avsola and Renflexis are lower-cost equivalents of the original Remicade, now widely used with comparable results. Drugs.com Monograph
  • Drug-level monitoring gets your dose right. Proactively checking infliximab trough levels and anti-drug antibodies — exactly what your Post-Infliximab tab tracks — helps keep more patients in remission. Annual TDM study (PMC)
  • Newer targets challenge anti-TNF like infliximab. IL-23 inhibitors (risankizumab, guselkumab) and the anti-TL1A drug tulisokibart are emerging as alternatives if an anti-TNF stops working or isn't tolerated. GI & Hepatology News
